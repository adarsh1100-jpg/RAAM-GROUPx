Interpretation & model boundaries

Nominal value not delivered = Original upfront incentives − (Revised upfront incentives + Wallet credits redeemed). This is not verified dealer cash savings, accounting expense, revenue or profit. Wallet issuance is discretionary, not mandatory on every MG or Toyota sale. Invoice-level redemption limits are illustrative and separate from the assumed lifetime redemption rate. Longer validity does not automatically imply higher redemption.
